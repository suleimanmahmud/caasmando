Our Institution

We are committed to excellence in agricultural education, animal production, research, and innovation. Our institution equips students with practical skills and scientific knowledge to drive sustainable agricultural development.

Who Uses Our Services?

The College of Agriculture and Animal Science provides educational, training, and research services to a wide range of individuals, organizations, and institutions involved in agriculture and livestock development.

Students and Aspiring Professionals

Our primary beneficiaries are students who desire to build careers in agriculture, animal production, veterinary support services, agribusiness, and agricultural technology. We equip them with practical skills, scientific knowledge, and entrepreneurial training needed to succeed in the modern agricultural industry.

Livestock Farmers and Farm Managers

Livestock farmers, poultry operators, dairy producers, and farm managers benefit from our training programs, consultancy services, and research findings. We help them improve animal health, increase productivity, apply modern breeding techniques, and adopt sustainable feeding practices.

Crop Producers and Agribusiness Entrepreneurs

Crop farmers and agribusiness owners use our services to gain knowledge in improved farming systems, soil management, pest control, and post-harvest handling. We support entrepreneurs who want to establish profitable agricultural ventures.

Agricultural Extension Workers and Consultants

Extension officers and agricultural consultants rely on our institution for updated research, training workshops, and professional development programs that enhance their ability to support farmers effectively.

Rural Communities

We also serve rural communities by providing outreach programs, agricultural awareness campaigns, and practical demonstrations that promote improved farming techniques and sustainable livestock management.

Secondary School Graduates and Career Seekers

Secondary school graduates and individuals seeking practical career opportunities in agriculture use our services to gain employable skills and professional certification. Through structured academic programs and hands-on training, we prepare young people for meaningful careers in livestock production, crop management, agricultural technology, and agribusiness entrepreneurship.

About US

The College of Agriculture and Animal Science is a leading institution dedicated to training skilled professionals in crop production, animal husbandry, agribusiness, and agricultural technology. Our programs combine classroom learning with hands-on practical experience on farms, laboratories, and research centers.
